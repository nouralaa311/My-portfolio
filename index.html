Beyond Notebook Prototypes: The 4 Engineering Pillars

Real-world AI systems fail when models leak future information, hallucinate numbers, or remain trapped in static notebooks. My engineering workflow is built around four production guarantees:

PILLAR 01 // ZERO DATA LEAKAGE

Leakage-Audited Pipelines & Walk-Forward Validation

Instead of relying on random train/test splits that inflate metrics through look-ahead bias, I engineer publication-lag-aware data joins, strict walk-forward temporal validation, causality testing, naive-baseline benchmarking, and statistical significance testing.

Walk-Forward Backtesting Lag-Aware Macroeconomic Joins Causality & Significance Tests

Hybrid RAG + Time-Series + Computer Vision + Geospatial AI
